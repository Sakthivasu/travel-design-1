Frequently Asked Questions.

Here are answers to a few common questions about getting in touch with VELMORA.

How can I make a reservation?

You can visit our Booking page, select your preferred room and dates, and submit your reservation request.

How can I enquire about special offers?

Visit our Offers page to explore available demonstration promotions. You can also contact us through the enquiry form.

Can I request assistance with a group booking?

Yes. Choose Group Booking in the enquiry form and provide your requirements in the message field.

How can I modify an existing booking?

Contact our guest assistance team by phone or email with your booking details to request a modification.

When will I receive a response?

Our team aims to respond to enquiries as soon as possible. Actual response times may vary.
